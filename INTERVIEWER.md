# Technical interview playbook
## Practical information
### Whiteboard
- For office interviews, book a room with a whiteboard
- For remote interview, use https://excalidraw.com/
### Laptop
If the candidate could not bring a laptop (or in case of technical difficulties), a laptop is available in the testing device locker. Login credentials are available in 1Password under `Macbook Technical ITW`.
### Sharing the `nodejs-assigment` repository
- Download the `main` branch by clicking [this link](https://github.com/hemnet/nodejs-assignment/archive/refs/heads/main.zip).
- At the beginning of the interview, send the zip file via Airdrop or email.
## Timeline suggestion for a 90 minutes interview
- Introduction (10 minutes max): 
	1. **Everyone** introduces themselves briefly
	2. **Interviewer** asks if candidate has read the Information for Candidates file sent by HR, and if there are any questions.
	3. **Interviewer** gives a quick summary of the interview format:
		- The focus is on problem solving and reasoning.
		- The whiteboard should be used when deemed necessary
		- Candidate should share their reasoning as often as possible: talk aloud, ask questions
		- An `ASSIGNMENT.md` file can be found at the root of the project, this is what we will be working on. The goal is to deliver a working, well-reasoned solution.
		- The candidate is free to use AI tools at their discretion, but we are assessing their skills, not the AI's.
		- The candidate can start when he's ready
- Feature request 1 (50 minutes max):
	- **Candidate** can (should) use the whiteboard and share their screen when implementing the feature.
- Feature request 2 (15 minutes max)
	- **Interviewer** asks the candidate to draw the high level solution on the whiteboard.
- Wrapping up the assignment (5 minutes max):
	- **Interviewer** asks the candidate what they would change to have this application production-ready.
- Conclusion (10 minutes max)
	- **Candidate** has the remaining time to ask questions to the interviewers.


## Tips for the interviewer
- Encourage the candidate to think aloud and share their reasoning
- Try to break the "interview" atmosphere by standing up next to the whiteboard with the candidate
- Don't hesitate to ask questions to the candidate to drive them if they get stuck
